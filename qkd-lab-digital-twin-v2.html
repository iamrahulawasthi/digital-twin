Guided laboratory procedure step 1 / 20
  1. Power ON Alice and Bob chassis.
  2. Initialize FPGA and wait for self-test.
  3. Lock the shared reference clock.
  4. Enable and verify the Alice-to-Bob synchronization path.
  5. Block Alice optical output and characterize SPD dark counts.
  6. Enable the gate generator and observe the timing reference.
  7. Enable TDC / FPGA acquisition.
  8. Enable controlled optical signal.
  9. Sweep gate delay and locate the signal-overlap region.
  10. Move the gate into the peak region and verify residual timing.
  11. Only then enable normal QKD time-tag acquisition.

  
  1. Associate detector events with pulse IDs.

  4. Reconcile BB84 basis labels.
  5. Estimate QBER from a public sample.
  6. Run LDPC-style error correction.
  7. Apply Toeplitz privacy amplification.
  8. Inspect the usable secret key.
  9. Generate the complete experiment report.
